Privacy Policy for Number Pop

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Number Pop does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data whatsoever. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind.

The app has no internet access

This is not a promise about how we behave — it is a property of the app. Number Pop requests no Android permissions at all, including the INTERNET permission. Without it the operating system will not allow the app to open a network connection, so no information can leave your device even in principle. Everything the game needs is contained inside the app itself.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of information locally, in your device's own browser storage, so that your progress survives closing the app:

  • Your high scores.
  • Which times tables and number ranges you have chosen, and your difficulty and sound settings.
  • A practice record of which individual number facts you have answered correctly, so the game can ask more of the ones you find hard.

None of this is personal information, none of it identifies anyone, and none of it ever leaves your device. It is not backed up to us, because there is no “us” for it to be sent to. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it permanently.

Children's privacy

Number Pop is designed for children and is intended to be safe for them by construction rather than by policy. It contains:

  • No advertising of any kind, and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, tracking, profiling or fingerprinting.
  • No account, sign-in, email capture or age gate — nothing to fill in.
  • No chat, messaging, user-generated content or contact with other players.
  • No links out of the app, no social media integration, and no in-app purchases.

Because no personal information is ever collected, the app complies with the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the E.U. General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) including its provisions on children's data, and Google Play's Families policy requirements.

Third parties

There are none. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no software development kits from advertising networks, and no embedded services. The only externally authored component is the Fredoka typeface, used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 and bundled inside the app — it is a font file, it runs no code, and it makes no requests.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the revised version will be posted at this same address with an updated date above. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be a clarification rather than a new use of your data.
